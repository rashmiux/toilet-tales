Research fellowship · July–December 2022 · Chennai

ToiletTales

The fallacy of science: the absence of proof is a proof of absence.

I was part of a research fellowship from July to December 2022. Over those months, public toilets were surveyed across the length and breadth of Chennai, about 1,100 in all.

From that work, I devised a way to score each toilet: a baseline WASH score. Chepauk–Triplicane was then picked as the sample area to showcase it, and that is the data on this site.

Chepauk–Triplicane toilets

Every public toilet in the constituency, mapped and checked. Functional toilets were scored on 25 water, sanitation and hygiene indicators, each rated green (2), yellow (1) or red (0).

            Data. WASH scores for public toilets, Chepauk–Triplicane constituency, Chennai (Toilet Lab, Public Health and Hygiene vertical), collected during a research fellowship from July to December 2022. The scoring tool was adapted from the WHO WASH FIT tool.

            Partners. Greater Chennai Corporation, Recycle Bin, Toilet Tales, Cheer NGO, IHE Delft, Netherlands, and Madras Medical College.

            Map. Streets, rail and water © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.

            Method. Each indicator is rated 0, 1 or 2, so the 25 indicators give a maximum of 50 points. The overall score is that total doubled, out of 100. The core score uses the 10 core indicators (maximum 20 points). One plumbing rating was recorded as 1.5.
